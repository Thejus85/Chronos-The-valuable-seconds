# chronos: the valuable second

## how to play

- move with `wasd` or the arrow keys. hold `shift` to run.
- press `e` near a person to talk.
- collect all four loom stabilizers in the facility and jungle.
- press `q` to change timelines. press `c` or `tab` to open the loom.
- after collecting all four stabilizers, enter the future for the fight.
- fight order: two drones, one drone, one more drone, then the chrono warden.
- aim with the mouse. hold `f` or left-click to fire a temporal pulse.
- if you are defeated, the fight starts again from the first two drones. your collected items stay safe.
- after the fight, talk to dr. aris, build the chronos matrix at the loom, then use the facility nexus portal.
- the ending brings the lab back online and secures the surviving timeline branches.

## getting started

open `project.godot` in godot 4.7 or newer, then run `main.tscn`.
