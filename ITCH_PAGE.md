# chronos: the valuable second

## tagline

change the past. steady the present. survive the future.

## description

one second can change an entire timeline.

explore a research facility and a hidden jungle, speak with the people caught between eras, and collect four loom stabilizers to bring the timelines back into balance. use the loom to shift through time, then carry what you find into a future under attack.

when the collector drones arrive, aim with your mouse and fire temporal pulses. face the drone waves and the chrono warden, then craft the chronos matrix and decide the fate of the surviving branches.

## controls

- move: wasd or arrow keys
- run: hold shift
- talk: press e near a character
- change timeline: press q
- open the loom: press c or tab
- aim and fire: mouse and left-click, or hold f

## getting started

open `project.godot` with godot 4.7 or newer and run `main.tscn`.
